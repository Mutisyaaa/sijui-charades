      📱 Install on iPhone / iPad

      Install Sijui to play in full-screen anytime, even without an internet connection!

      1. Tap Share: In Safari's bottom toolbar, tap the Share icon (⎋ or square with upward arrow).
      2. Tap "Add to Home Screen": Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen (➕).
      3. Tap "Add": Confirm in the top-right corner.

      Open Sijui from your home screen for the full app experience!
